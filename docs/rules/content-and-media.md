# Content and media rules

- Store only owned, suitably licensed, or correctly attributed exercise images.
- Do not use Basic-Fit or GymVisual assets without explicit suitable permission.
- Do not generate exercise animations with AI.
- Store image source, license, and attribution alongside each asset.
- Store external YouTube URLs only; never copy or rehost videos.
- Open YouTube links in a new tab with safe external-link attributes.
- Include a clear statement that GymAlternatief does not provide medical or injury advice.
- Human-review every pilot exercise, equipment relation, and important alternative.
